Community Safety

Tips for Safe & Seamless Errands

Urgent.ng connects you directly with independent local runners. Here is how to keep every errand smooth, reliable, and secure.

Pay Only on Delivery

Never pay for errand labor upfront. Inspect your delivered package or shopping purchase with official receipts before transferring funds or handing over cash.

Check Ratings & Reviews

Before accepting a bid, review the runner's completed task count, star ratings, and honest feedback left by other community members in your area.

Use Public Landmarks

For handovers, choose well-lit public spots like shopping malls, fuel stations, or office gates. Keep all errand terms and expectations documented in in-app chat.
